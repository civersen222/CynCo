SPEC (verbatim, from docs/civkings-redesign-spec.md)

  3.1 The Ladder
  - Rank across the four axes (`_axis_capital`, `_axis_standing`, `_axis_blood`,
    `_axis_world`) is computed for ALL houses every turn and the overall rank is
    ALWAYS public.
  - The WHY is earned: per-axis breakdowns, trajectories, and rival agendas are
    gated behind the existing 4-tier intel fog (blind -> mood -> intent -> depth;
    sources: border, ties, assets, informant).
  - Victory: the century ends (existing `TURN_BUDGET`); the house atop the ladder
    wins the age, with `judge()`'s epilogues ("Hegemon of the Age", "The Long
    Ledger", ...) keyed to how they won. Extinction/revolution endings remain as
    loss states.

  8. Consequence beats (how results reach the player)
  1. Signature (instant): the player's act is acknowledged on the spot - ink,
     stamp, sound.
  2. Season (End Turn): the Gazette front page prints above the fold with the
     turn's true headlines.
  3. Inquiry ("why?"): every changed number chains back through `provenance.py`
     Cause/Attributed - wired to ALL systems, not just legitimacy. `.check()`
     guarantees causes sum to the delta.
  4. Deflection: when the world thwarts the player, the thwart has an
     institutional FACE and a paper trail. Never a silent fizzle, never an
     unexplained dice roll. (Until campaign C3 ships the full Orders, valid
     faces are the union Movement leader (society/labor.py) and the Master of
     the Press (papers.py).)

REQUIRED API SURFACE (the sealed gate consumes exactly this)
- game.ladder.standings() -> list[(house:str, rank:int, axes:dict[str,float])]
  All houses; ranks a strict total order 1..N from the four endings.py axes.
- game.beats.log -> list[Beat]; Beat has .kind in {"signature","season",
  "inquiry","deflection"}, .turn:int, .text:str, .face:str|None,
  .provenance:Attributed|None
- game.beats.deltas(turn) -> list[(label:str, att:Attributed)]
  EVERY player-visible numeric delta for that turn. deltas() IS the render
  source: the Ledger and Gazette must draw their numbers from it (campaign C4
  will assert this wiring structurally; build it that way now).
- game.beats.inquire(label, turn) -> Attributed  (the "why?" chain)
- game.acts.* : ALL player verbs route through this module and each emits an
  instant signature beat. Minimum for this campaign: game.acts.set_dial(eid,
  value) (wraps Enterprise.extraction_dial).
- gilded.ui.registry with:
    VERBS: dict[verb_id -> {"what":str, "why_now":str, "serves":str}]
      (every acts.* verb registered; all three fields non-empty; "serves" names
       a ladder axis or ambition)
    DATA: dict[datum_id -> screen_id]  (one owner screen per datum; seed it
      with everything you add this campaign)
