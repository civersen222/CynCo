DESTINATION
The player picks the same kind of Goal the AI plays, the court gains private
wants that back or oppose it, and the House screen shows all of it. This is
campaign 2 of 5 of the CivKings redesign: the game stops being a diorama and
gives the player a stake.

SPEC (verbatim, from docs/civkings-redesign-spec.md)

  3.2 Ambitions — both levels, in tension
  - House ambition (player-chosen): the player picks a Goal from the same 7
    families the AI uses (Conquest, Dominion, Buyout, Dynasty, Intrigue, Glory,
    Consolidation) with the same 10-turn commit and honest levers. Symmetry:
    rivals read the player's agenda through THEIR fog, exactly as the player
    reads theirs.
  - Character wants (derived — the one new mechanic): each family member derives
    a private want from their 30 dispositions. Wants align or clash with the
    house ambition, yielding a stance: backs / wary / opposes.
  - Completing an ambition pays ladder movement on its natural axis and unlocks
    the next choice.

  4. House screen: the Court in Session
  - Ambition banner on top: seal, family, target, why-string, commit clock
    ("turn 3 of 10").
  - Court grid: portrait cards — name, age, relation, small-caps traits, stance
    badge, and their one-line want citing the driving disposition.
  - Stances are COMPUTED from dispositions vs. the ambition — never random,
    always inspectable.

REQUIRED API SURFACE (the sealed gate consumes exactly this — every name, every
signature, every field, every string, verbatim; wave 1 of C1 was lost to shape
divergence, do not repeat it)
- game.set_ambition(house, family, target=None)
  Writes the player's Goal into game.agendas — the SAME store the AI's agendas
  live in and gilded/intel.py reads from (this is what makes fog symmetry free:
  with an informant + ties, intel.report(...).apparent_intent must contain
  f"Pursuing {family}" with NO changes to intel.py). The stored goal must have
  .family == family and .commit_turns == 10. target is a house name (used by
  e.g. Buyout) or None.
- game.ambitions.status(house) -> dict with keys: family, target, turns_left,
  progress, fulfilled. turns_left decrements once per end_turn; fulfilled is
  None while the clock runs and becomes True or False (a real evaluation of the
  family's win condition — honest levers, no coin flips) when it reaches 0.
  The evaluation emits a beat whose .text contains the word "ambition"; a
  FULFILLED ambition pays ladder movement on its natural axis, and that delta
  appears in game.beats.deltas(game.resolved_turn) with a Cause whose label
  contains "ambition".
- Every ADULT character (age >= 16, in game.realms[house].characters) gets
  character.want -> {"text": str (non-empty, one line),
                     "disposition": a key present in character.dispositions,
                     "stance": one of "backs" | "wary" | "opposes"}
  Deterministic given seed (two boots of seed 7 produce byte-identical wants).
  Stances are DERIVED from the 30 dispositions vs. the active ambition family —
  across ordinary seeds all three stances occur; a court that always agrees is
  a calibration failure the gate checks for.
- gilded.ui.house module (new) with:
    court_cards(game, house) -> list[dict], one card per adult, keys:
      "cid" (character id), "traits" (== character.traits),
      "stance" (== character.want["stance"]), "want_text" (== character.want["text"])
      Cards mirror the model EXACTLY — same set of ids, same values.
    banner(game, house) -> dict with "family" (the active ambition family) and
      "clock" == f"turn {10 - turns_left + 1} of 10" at read time
      ("turn 1 of 10" immediately after set_ambition; "turn 4 of 10" after
      three end_turns).
- Wire it into the House tab: the Court in Session renders from court_cards()
  and banner() (the gate checks the functions; campaign C4 will assert the
  screen wiring structurally — build it that way now).
- Register every new player verb (set_ambition at minimum) in
  gilded.ui.registry.VERBS with non-empty what/why_now/serves, and route it
  through game.acts so it emits an instant signature beat.
